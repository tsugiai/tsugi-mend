# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant v2.1 by reference:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

We pledge to make participation in `tsugi-mend` a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, level of
experience, or viewpoint. Contributors are expected to communicate with care,
assume good faith, and focus disagreement on the work rather than the person.

For enforcement questions or reports, contact the public maintainer:

Tong Liu <tong@tsugicinema.com>
